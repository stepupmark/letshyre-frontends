Say you run a 40-person design agency in Ahmedabad. You hire three times a year: a designer in
March, a developer in July, a project manager in November. Each search takes about a month. For the
other nine months, you don't open a hiring tool at all.

A monthly subscription charges you for all twelve. LetsHyre charges for the candidates you choose to
meet, when you choose to meet them. For a team that hires now and then, that's the difference
between paying for a tool and paying for hires.

## How LetsHyre credits work

Credits are the only thing on LetsHyre that costs money, and they're used for one thing: unlocking a
candidate's AI interview.

- **One credit unlocks one interview**, with the candidate's contact details, full scorecard,
  recording and resume. Everything you can see before that is free.
- **Credits are bought once.** Nothing renews, and you're never charged automatically.
- **Credits never expire.** Credits bought in March are still there in November.
- **Every credit costs the same**, whichever plan you buy. Bigger plans give you more credits and
  more teammates, not cheaper or better unlocks.
- **New accounts start with ten free credits**, enough to try it on a real role.

The agency reads the free signals on each candidate and unlocks four interviews per role. That's
twelve credits for the year. The first ten came free with the account, so the year's hiring costs
two credits. Next year, it costs twelve, bought when they're needed.

Compare that with what a monthly plan asks: a bill every month, including the nine months with no
open role, and a cancellation to remember if you want it to stop.

Today's price per credit is on the [pricing page](/en/pricing). Prices are in rupees, GST is added
at checkout, and you can pay by UPI, card or net banking.

## What actually changes between plans

Every plan unlocks exactly the same information. What changes is the number of credits, how many
teammates can see results, and support.

| Plan     | Teammates who can view results | Top-ups      |
| -------- | ------------------------------ | ------------ |
| Free     | 1                              | Not included |
| Pro      | 5                              | Included     |
| Business | 25                             | Included     |
| Custom   | Any team size                  | Custom       |

So pick a plan for its seats and the number of credits you'll use, not for features. For the
agency, the hiring manager and one partner review candidates together, so Free covers them, and
they'd move to Pro if a third person joined the panel.

## When credits cost more than they should

Credits only stay cheap if you spend them on purpose.

- **Opening the ten best matches by habit.** Read the six free signals first; we've written up
  [how to shortlist before you unlock](/en/blogs/before-you-unlock).
- **Unlocking every interview a candidate has.** Each costs a credit. Start with the one closest
  to your role, which is marked for you.
- **Buying ahead of a role you haven't opened.** Credits don't expire, but there's no reason to
  pay for them early. Top up when the role is live.

Refunds are limited to mistakes such as duplicate payments, and unlocked profiles can't be refunded,
which is one more reason to unlock with care. The [refund policy](/en/refund-policy) has the detail.

## When a bigger plan makes sense

If you hire every month, or several people review every candidate, Business gives you more credits
and 25 seats. If you're an agency or hire at volume, a custom plan sizes credits and seats to you.
Either way, it's still a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

## Before your next role

Work out two numbers: how many roles you'll hire this year, and how many interviews you'll unlock
for each. Multiply them, subtract the ten free credits, and that's what a year of hiring needs. For
the agency, it's two.
