Follow-up messages after an interview
From the LetsHyre blog: letshyre.ai/en/blogs/ghosted-after-final-round
Replace the words in [brackets]. Send to the recruiter by email unless the message says otherwise.


1. First follow-up (one working day after the date they gave, or five working days if none)

Subject: [Role] interview on [day]

Hi [recruiter],

Thank you again for organising [day]'s round. I enjoyed [one specific part, e.g. the design discussion with Priya]. Is there an update on the timeline? I'm still very interested in the role.

Thanks,
[Your name]


2. Second follow-up (a week after the first)

Subject: Re: [Role] interview on [day]

Hi [recruiter],

Following up on my note from last week. Since we spoke I've [one new thing: thought more about the data pipeline question / finished the project I mentioned], and I'm keen to keep talking.

Could you let me know where things stand, even if the decision isn't made yet?

Thanks,
[Your name]


3. When you hold another offer (only if it's real)

Subject: [Role]: a timeline question

Hi [recruiter],

I wanted to let you know that I've received an offer from another company, and I need to give them an answer by [date]. [Company] is my first choice, so I'd like to hear from you before then if that's possible.

If your process needs longer, I understand. Please let me know either way.

Thanks,
[Your name]


4. Closing message (after two follow-ups and about three weeks)

Subject: Re: [Role] interview on [day]

Hi [recruiter],

I haven't heard back, so I'll assume the role has moved on without me. I enjoyed meeting the team and would be glad to hear about future roles. Thank you for your help.

Best,
[Your name]
