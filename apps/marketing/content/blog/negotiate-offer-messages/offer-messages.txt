Offer negotiation messages
From the LetsHyre blog: letshyre.ai/en/blogs/negotiate-offer-messages
Replace the names and numbers in [brackets]. Read each one aloud before you send it.


1. Thank them, and move it to email (WhatsApp or chat)

Thank you, that's great news and I'm really glad. Could you email me the offer letter with the full salary breakup? I'll go through it carefully and come back to you by [day].


2. When they push for a quick answer

I understand you'd like to close this quickly, and so would I. I want to give the offer the attention it deserves, so [day] is the earliest I can respond properly. If that doesn't work for your timeline, please let me know.


3. When the breakup is missing

Thanks for sending this. Before I respond, could you share the breakup: fixed pay, variable pay and how much of last year's variable paid out, PF, and whether gratuity or a joining bonus is counted in the [CTC]?


4. The counter (email)

Subject: Offer for [role], [your name]

Hi [recruiter],

Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about the role, and about [one specific thing] in particular.

Having gone through the breakup, I'd like to ask whether the fixed pay could move to [amount]. [One reason based on the role's scope or the market.] If the fixed pay is hard to move, I'd be glad to talk about [a joining bonus / an early review / another option] instead.

I'd like to make this work, and I'm happy to get on a call if that's easier.

Best,
[your name]


5. When they say it's the best they can do

Thank you for checking, I appreciate it. If the fixed pay can't move, would it be possible to [bring the first salary review forward to six months, with the criteria agreed now / one other request]?


6. Accepting, in writing

Hi [recruiter], thank you for working through this with me. I'm happy to accept. To confirm what we agreed: fixed pay of [amount], variable of [amount], [any bonus or other terms], and a joining date of [date]. Could you send the revised offer letter with these terms? I'll sign as soon as it arrives.


7. Saying no, kindly

Hi [recruiter], thank you for the offer and for your time over the last few weeks. After a lot of thought, I've decided not to take it up. I've enjoyed every conversation with the team, and I'd be glad to stay in touch.
