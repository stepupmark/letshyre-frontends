Open with the answer. Two or three sentences that tell the reader what they'll get and who it's for,
before any background. Someone who reads only this paragraph should still leave with the main point.

## One idea per section

Each `##` heading is one idea, written as a plain statement a reader could skim. Keep paragraphs to
three or four sentences. Link a claim to
[the study that backs it up](https://press.roberthalf.com/2016-08-11-Are-You-Taking-Too-Long-To-Hire),
and the link is listed under the post as a numbered source.

Link to the rest of the site with a path, like [the employers page](/en/employers).

### A smaller heading inside a section

Use `###` only inside a `##` section, never on its own.
