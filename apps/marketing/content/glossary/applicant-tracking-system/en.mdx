---
term: Applicant tracking system
short: The software employers use to collect applications, move candidates through stages and keep hiring records. Often called an ATS.
aliases: [ATS, applicant tracking]
---

An applicant tracking system, or ATS, is where an employer's applications land. Recruiters use it to
read resumes, move candidates between stages, schedule interviews and keep notes.

Most of the sorting is still done by people. Many systems can filter on knock-out questions or
search by keyword, but a recruiter usually decides who goes through. That's why a resume written
for a person, with plain section headings and the words the job ad uses, works better than one
written to trick a machine. [Your resume after AI](/en/blogs/resume-after-ai) has more.
