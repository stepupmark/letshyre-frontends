---
term: Background verification
short: Checks an employer runs, with your consent, on the employment, education, address and records you've declared.
aliases: [BGV, background check, background checks, background screening]
---

Background verification, usually called BGV in India, confirms what a candidate has told an
employer. Most checks cover past employment, education, address and criminal records. Some add
references, identity, credit or a review of PF records through the
[UAN](/en/blogs/glossary/uan).

Employers often hand the work to a verification firm and make the offer conditional on a clear
report. The candidate signs a consent form first, listing which checks will be run.
