---
term: Counter-offer
short: What your current employer offers, usually more money, to keep you after you've resigned or said you're leaving.
aliases: [counter offer, counteroffer]
---

A counter-offer is your employer's attempt to keep you once you've said you're going. It's usually a
raise, sometimes a new title or a different team.

It can fix the pay and leave everything else that made you look elsewhere unchanged. Decide whether
you'd stay for one before you resign. Accepting a new offer and then taking a counter-offer near your
joining date costs you goodwill with that company and its recruiter.
