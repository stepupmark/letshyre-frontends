---
term: CTC
short: Cost to company. Everything an employer spends on you in a year, including money that never reaches your bank account.
aliases: [cost to company]
---

CTC is the number Indian employers quote in an offer: the total they expect to spend on you in a
year. It's not your salary, and it's not what you take home.

A typical CTC holds four kinds of money. **Fixed pay** is your basic salary and allowances, paid
monthly. **Variable pay** depends on targets and may not pay out in full. **Employer PF** goes into
your provident fund, not your account. **Gratuity** is usually paid only if you stay five years or
more.

So two offers with the same CTC can leave you with very different monthly pay. Ask for the full
breakdown, and compare offers on [fixed pay](/en/blogs/glossary/fixed-pay) instead of the total.
[Negotiating your offer in India](/en/blogs/negotiate-offer-india) walks through one line by line.
