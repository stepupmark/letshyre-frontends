---
term: Fixed pay
short: "The part of your pay that arrives every month whatever happens: basic salary plus allowances such as HRA, before deductions."
aliases: [fixed salary, fixed component]
---

Fixed pay is the guaranteed part of a [CTC](/en/blogs/glossary/ctc). It's usually your basic salary
plus allowances such as house rent allowance, paid in monthly instalments.

It's still not take-home pay. Your own provident fund contribution, professional tax and income tax
come out of it first. But unlike [variable pay](/en/blogs/glossary/variable-pay), it doesn't depend
on anyone's targets, which makes it the fairest number to compare two offers on.
