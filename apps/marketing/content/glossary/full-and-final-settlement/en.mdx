---
term: Full and final settlement
short: The last payment from an employer when you leave, covering salary to your last day, unused leave, dues and deductions.
aliases: [F&F, full and final, final settlement]
---

The full and final settlement, often called F&F, closes your account with an employer. It usually
covers salary up to your last working day, encashment of earned leave, any bonus you're owed,
reimbursements, and deductions such as a notice shortfall or company property you haven't returned.

The Code on Wages, in force since November 2025, says wages due must be paid within two working
days of leaving. Many employers still run a longer settlement cycle, so ask HR for the date and a
written breakup, and check it against your last payslip. [Gratuity](/en/blogs/glossary/gratuity),
if you're owed it, is often paid separately.
