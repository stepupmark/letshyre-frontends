---
term: Joining bonus
short: A one-time payment for accepting an offer, usually paid with the first salary and often repayable if you leave within a year.
aliases: [joining bonuses, signing bonus, sign-on bonus]
---

A joining bonus is a single payment an employer makes for taking the job, often to cover a bonus
you're giving up or a buyout of your notice period. It's taxed as salary in the month it's paid.

Most offers that include one also include a clause saying you'll repay it, in full or pro-rata, if
you resign within a set period, usually 12 months. Read that clause before you count the bonus as
yours.
