---
term: Notice buyout
short: Paying your employer for the notice days you don't serve, so you can leave early. Allowed only if they agree.
aliases: [buyout, notice period buyout]
---

A notice buyout lets you leave before your [notice period](/en/blogs/glossary/notice-period) ends
by paying for the days you don't serve. A common formula is the unserved days times a day's pay,
where a day's pay is a month's pay divided by 30. Your employer's policy decides whether that's
basic or gross pay, which changes the number a lot.

Your current employer doesn't have to accept one. If your new employer offers to cover it, get that
in the offer letter, with when they'll pay it and any conditions, and ask how it will be taxed.
