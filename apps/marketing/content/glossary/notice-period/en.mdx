---
term: Notice period
short: How long you keep working after you resign. In India it's set by your contract and is often 60 or 90 days.
aliases: [notice periods]
---

A notice period is the time between handing in your resignation and your last working day. Your
employment contract sets it, and in India 60 or 90 days is common for experienced roles, sometimes
more for senior ones.

It shapes a job search. Employers count forward from the day you'd resign, so a long notice can
cost you offers from teams that need someone soon. If you're already serving it, say the date you
can join, not the length of your notice. Some employers allow an early exit through a
[notice buyout](/en/blogs/glossary/notice-buyout).

[Serving your notice period?](/en/blogs/notice-period-job-hunt) covers how to plan the months.
