---
term: Relieving letter
short: A letter from your old employer confirming you've left and been released from your duties. Many Indian employers ask for it when you join.
aliases: [relieving date, relieving]
---

A relieving letter confirms that your employer accepted your resignation and that you've been
released on a given date. Many Indian employers ask for it, along with an experience letter, before
or soon after you join.

The relieving date is the one that matters for your next job, so get it confirmed in writing before
you agree a joining date. If your employer delays the letter, ask HR in writing, and tell your new
employer early rather than on your first day.
