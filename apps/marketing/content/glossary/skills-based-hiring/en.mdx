---
term: Skills-based hiring
short: Hiring on evidence that someone can do the work, rather than on degrees, job titles or years of experience.
aliases: [skills-first hiring, hiring for skills]
---

Skills-based hiring means deciding who to hire from what they can show they do, not from proxies
like a degree or a past employer's name.

Dropping a degree requirement from a job ad is the easy part. It only changes who gets hired when
the process actually checks the skill, with a work sample, a [structured
interview](/en/blogs/glossary/structured-interview) or a scored assessment.
[Everyone says they hire for skills](/en/blogs/skills-over-degrees) looks at why most employers
haven't got there yet.
