---
term: Structured interview
short: An interview where every candidate gets the same questions in the same order, scored against the same rubric.
aliases: [structured interviews]
---

In a structured interview, the questions are decided before anyone is interviewed, every candidate
hears the same ones, and each answer is scored against a written rubric. Interviewers write their
scores before they compare notes.

That consistency is the point. When every candidate answers the same questions, you're comparing
people, not conversations. Research on selection methods has repeatedly found structured interviews
among the better predictors of job performance, and well ahead of unstructured chats.
[How to talk about your work](/en/blogs/talk-about-your-work) looks at it from the candidate's side.
