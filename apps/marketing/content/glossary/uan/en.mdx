---
term: UAN
short: Your Universal Account Number with the EPFO. It stays the same across employers and records every PF contribution made for you.
aliases: [Universal Account Number, UAN number]
---

The Universal Account Number is your lifelong provident fund identity. Each employer that covers
you under the EPF scheme links your PF account to the same UAN, so your service history and balance
follow you from job to job.

You can see that history yourself on the EPFO member portal: each employer, and the months they
paid contributions for. Background verification firms can also read it, with your consent, which
is why a job left off a CV usually shows up anyway.
