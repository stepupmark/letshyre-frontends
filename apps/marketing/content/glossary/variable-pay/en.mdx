---
term: Variable pay
short: Pay that depends on targets, yours, your team's or the company's. It can pay out in full, in part or not at all.
aliases: [variable component, performance bonus]
---

Variable pay is the part of a [CTC](/en/blogs/glossary/ctc) that's tied to performance. It's often
paid once or twice a year, and the offer letter rarely says how much of it people actually received
last year.

Before you count it, ask three things: what the targets are, who sets them, and what share of the
variable people in the same role were paid in the last cycle. Until you know, treat it as a maybe and
compare offers on [fixed pay](/en/blogs/glossary/fixed-pay).
