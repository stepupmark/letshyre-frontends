Changing jobs after a few years of experience is a different search from your first one. Employers
read less of your resume and ask more about how you work. The offer comes as a [CTC](/en/blogs/glossary/ctc)
with parts you won't see every month, and a 60 or 90-day [notice
period](/en/blogs/glossary/notice-period) decides when you can actually start.

These posts follow a search in the order it happens. Start with what your resume needs to say now
that every resume sounds polished, and how to talk about a gap in it. Then how to show you're ready
for a bigger role before you have the title. Finish with reading and negotiating the offer, and
making the months of your notice work for you.
