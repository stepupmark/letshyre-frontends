Most hiring processes are slower and less accurate than the people running them think. Days go to
waiting rather than deciding, and the shortlist is still built from degrees and job titles that say
little about whether someone can do the work.

Start with where the days go and what they cost you in candidates. Then read why dropping a degree
requirement isn't the same as hiring for skills, and what checking a skill actually takes.
