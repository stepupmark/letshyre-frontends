Two things have changed hiring in the last couple of years. Candidates write their applications with
AI, so resumes and cover letters read alike. And employers use AI to sort them, while still keeping
the decision with people.

Read these in order. The first looks at why a polished resume tells an employer so little now. The
second at how hiring teams actually use AI, and where they draw the line. The last puts both against
the Indian market in late 2026: which roles are in demand, which aren't, and what that means if
you're hiring or looking.
